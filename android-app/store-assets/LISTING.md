## English (en-US), default

**App name (30):**
Nestling: Baby Log for Parents

**Short description (80):**
Track feeds, sleep and diapers together. Your coparent sees every entry live.

**Full description:**

Nestling is a baby log built for two parents. Log a feed, a nap or a diaper on your phone, and your coparent sees it on theirs right away, so you both always know when the baby last ate, slept or was changed.

LOG IN ONE TAP
• Breastfeeding with a left and right timer, bottles in oz or ml, solids and pumping.
• Sleep with a live timer, naps and night sleep.
• Wet and dirty diapers, and potty training.
• Growth, teeth, temperature, medicine, symptoms, doctor visits and vaccines.
• Tummy time, bath, walks, and your own trackers, like vitamin D drops.

SHARED WITH YOUR COPARENT
• Invite your coparent, a grandparent or a caregiver with a one-time code.
• Every entry shows who logged it.
• Give someone view-only access when they only need to look.

SEE WHAT'S COMING UP
• The next nap window, based on typical awake times for your baby's age.
• When the next feed is usually due, based on your last 3 days.
• Upcoming doctor appointments, with details you can copy and send.
• The next well-baby checkup on the American Academy of Pediatrics schedule.

UNDERSTAND THE PATTERNS
• Today so far: pick the stats you care about.
• A 24-hour timeline for each day.
• 7, 14 and 30-day charts for sleep, feeds, milk and diapers.
• Growth charts with the WHO median as a reference line.
• A teeth chart based on the American Dental Association's timeline.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Eight warm themes, each with a light and dark version for night feeds.
• Five icon styles, including emoji.
• Arrange the log tiles in the order you use them.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL
• No ads and no tracking.
• Save a backup or export a spreadsheet for your pediatrician.
• Delete your account any time, in the app or on the web.

Nestling is a log, not medical advice. Nap windows, growth reference lines and fever notes are general information from public sources (Cleveland Clinic, the World Health Organization, the American Academy of Pediatrics, the American Dental Association). Always follow your pediatrician's guidance.

## Play Console answers

**App access:** All features need an account. Provide a review account (email + password) with a baby already added, under App content → App access.

**Ads:** No ads.

**Content rating questionnaire:** Pick the category for utility or productivity apps (not games or social networks). No violence, sexual content, profanity, drugs, gambling or user-generated content shared publicly. Users share logs only with people they invite. Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.

**Target audience and content:** 18 and over only. The app is for parents and caregivers; children don't use it. This keeps it out of the Families program.

**Data safety:**
- Data collected: Personal info → Email address, Name (the family display name) · Health and fitness → Health info (the baby's logs). Optional: none of it is used for ads or analytics.
- Purpose: App functionality and Account management.
- Shared with third parties: No (Supabase is a service provider processing data on our behalf, which Play doesn't count as sharing).
- Encrypted in transit: Yes.
- Users can request deletion: Yes, in the app and at the account deletion URL above.

**Health apps declaration** (App content → Health apps): Nestling has health features. Closest categories: Nutrition and Weight Management (feeding), Sleep Management (sleep), Activity and Fitness (growth measurements), Medication and Treatment Management (medicine log), and health status monitoring (temperature). It doesn't connect to medical devices and isn't a regulated medical device.

**Account deletion:** In-app at Settings → Account → Delete my account; web at the account deletion URL above.

**Testing:** If the app is published from the Lexcellent Solutions LLC organization account (like Haiti Quiz Challenge and Paskle), the 12-tester, 14-day closed test for new personal accounts doesn't apply. Still start with Internal testing.
