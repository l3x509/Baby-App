Bebenou Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Lexcellent Solutions LLC

Bebenou helps parents and caregivers keep a shared log of a baby's feeds, sleep, diapers, growth and health. This policy covers the Bebenou website and the Bebenou Android app, both run by Lexcellent Solutions LLC ("we").

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; we never see them. We use your email only to sign you in and, when you ask, to send a password reset link through our email delivery provider. If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link with us so we can create your account; we don’t get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Your name in the family: the name you choose to show to the people you share a log with, such as "Mom" or "Grandpa".
  • Your baby's details: the name, birth date, sex (only if you choose to enter it, for the growth reference line) and the unit system you pick.
  • What you log: feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping, solids, growth measurements (including head, belly and chest size), teeth, temperatures, vitals (heart rate, breathing, oxygen, blood pressure), jaundice checks, medicines, symptoms, doctor visits, vaccines, newborn screenings, activities, notes and milestones. Some of this is health information about your baby.
  • Photos you attach: if you add a photo to an entry (for example a rash, a stool or a milestone), it is stored in a private storage area that only members of that baby's log can open. Photos are shrunk before upload and are not shared publicly.
  • Subscription status: if you subscribe to Premium, our payment partner RevenueCat and Google Play handle the purchase. We receive whether the subscription is active and when it ends, linked to your account; we never see your card details.

Settings such as your language, theme, icon style, clock format and log tiles are saved only on your device. On Android, the home-screen widget reads a short summary (last feed, diaper and sleep) that the app saves on your phone.

Bebenou has no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not use your information to build advertising profiles.

How we use it

  • To show your baby's log and sync it between the people you invite.
  • To calculate what the app shows you, such as daily totals, nap windows and growth charts. This happens in the app.
  • To answer you if you contact us.

Ask Bebenou

  • Common questions are answered from guides built into the app. Nothing is sent anywhere for these.
  • When you ask for a personal answer, your question and a short summary of the last 7 days of your baby's log (age in days, feed and diaper counts, recent health entries) are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the answer. Your baby's name, your name and your email are not included.
  • Questions and answers aren't saved to your account. If you report an answer, the question, answer and reason are saved so we can review them.
  • Answers are general information, not medical advice.

Who can see it

  • People you invite: anyone who joins a baby's log with an invite code can see everything in that log, and can add entries unless they were invited as view-only. Each person's name appears next to the entries they add.
  • Our service providers: data and photos are stored with Supabase, which hosts our database, file storage and sign-in. RevenueCat and Google Play process Premium purchases. When you use Ask Bebenou's personal answers, Anthropic processes your question and log summary to write the answer. They process data only to provide these services to us.
  • No one else: we do not sell or rent your information or share it with advertisers. We would disclose it only if the law requires us to.

How it's protected

Data travels over encrypted connections (https). Database access rules ensure each signed-in person can read only the logs they belong to.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep your information while your account exists.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Account → Delete my account) or on our account deletion page. Deletion is immediate.
  • When you delete your account, your sign-in and your name are removed. A log only you use is deleted with all its entries and photos. A log someone else also uses stays with them: entries you added remain in it for the family, without your name.
  • You can also remove individual entries, or leave a shared log, at any time in the app.
  • You can download a copy of a log at any time with Settings → Backup & restore.

Children

Bebenou is meant for parents and caregivers aged 18 and older. Children do not use it or create accounts. The information about a baby is entered by their parent or caregiver, who controls it and can delete it at any time.

Not medical advice

Bebenou is a log. Its nap windows, growth percentiles and reference lines, alerts, usual ranges and fever note are general information from public sources, not medical advice. Always follow your pediatrician's guidance.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
