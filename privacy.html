Nestling Baby Log ("Nestling") helps parents and caregivers keep a shared log of a baby's feeds, sleep, diapers, growth and health. This policy covers the Nestling website and the Nestling Android app, both run by Lexcellent Solutions LLC ("we").

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; we never see them.
  • Your name in the family: the name you choose to show to the people you share a log with, such as "Mom" or "Grandpa".
  • Your baby's details: the name, birth date, sex (only if you choose to enter it, for the growth reference line) and the unit system you pick.
  • What you log: feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping, solids, growth measurements, teeth, temperatures, medicines, symptoms, doctor visits, vaccines, activities, notes and milestones. Some of this is health information about your baby.

Settings such as your theme, icon style, clock format and log tiles are saved only on your device.

Nestling has no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not use your information to build advertising profiles.

How we use it

  • To show your baby's log and sync it between the people you invite.
  • To calculate what the app shows you, such as daily totals, nap windows and growth charts. This happens in the app.
  • To answer you if you contact us.

Who can see it

  • People you invite: anyone who joins a baby's log with an invite code can see everything in that log, and can add entries unless they were invited as view-only. Each person's name appears next to the entries they add.
  • Our service provider: data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and sign-in. Supabase processes it only to provide that service to us.
  • No one else: we do not sell or rent your information or share it with advertisers. We would disclose it only if the law requires us to.

How it's protected

Data travels over encrypted connections (https). Database access rules ensure each signed-in person can read only the logs they belong to.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep your information while your account exists.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Account → Delete my account) or on our account deletion page. Deletion is immediate.
  • When you delete your account, your sign-in and your name are removed. A log only you use is deleted with all its entries. A log someone else also uses stays with them: entries you added remain in it for the family, without your name.
  • You can also remove individual entries, or leave a shared log, at any time in the app.
  • You can download a copy of a log at any time with Settings → Backup & restore.

Children

Nestling is meant for parents and caregivers aged 18 and older. Children do not use it or create accounts. The information about a baby is entered by their parent or caregiver, who controls it and can delete it at any time.

Not medical advice

Nestling is a log. Its nap windows, growth reference lines and fever note are general information from public sources, not medical advice. Always follow your pediatrician's guidance.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
