You can delete your account here without the app, or in the app under Settings → Account → Delete my account.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password) and your name in every baby's log.
  • Any baby's log that only you use, with all of its entries.

What stays

  • A baby's log that someone else also uses stays with them, and they become its owner. Entries you added stay in that log for them, without your name.

Deletion is immediate and can't be undone. To keep a copy, open the app first and use Settings → Backup & restore → Save backup.
